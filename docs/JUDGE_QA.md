## Top 3 Risks for Judges (Executive Summary)

1. **Pretraining Exposure vs. True Domain Generalisation (`Cholec80` / `CholecSeg8k` vs. `Endoscapes2023`):** Because `Cholec80` (2016) and `CholecSeg8k` (2020) are public benchmarks that may exist in frontier VLM pretraining corpora (`docs/research/eval-leakage-traps.md`), our primary generalisation proof relies on DUA-gated `Endoscapes2023` (`out/eval_audit.json`, `n=148` scorable labels across `10` unseen test videos, where verification lifts accuracy from `67.57%` to `70.50%` [`+2.94 pp`, $p=0.9965$] at `93.92%` coverage, with `gallbladder` at `98.61%` accepted accuracy but `cystic_duct` dropping to `40.30%` when buried in fat) and disjoint `CholecT50` videos (`n=240` across `40` videos, `89.58% -> 91.23%` [`+1.64 pp`, $p=0.990$] at `95.00%` coverage).
2. **Same-Family Verifier Error Correlation ("Gemini Checking Gemini"):** Published evaluations show two LLMs from the same family share up to `~60%` of wrong answers (`arXiv:2506.07962` in `docs/research/verified-labels-lap-chole.md`), and our own cross-layer audit (`narrator/out/eval/RESULTS.md`, `n=32`) measured a `1.60×` correlated-miss ratio between label-table and blind Gemini visual checks (`L4 × L5`) while zero-shot `OpenCLIP` (`L5c`) scored at chance (`52.0%` on `n=200` GT cases); we mitigate this via zoomed crop-tightening in `gemini-4-argon`, deterministic anatomical/temporal rules (`L1–L3, L7`), and multi-pass spatial consensus (`out/vote_experiment.json`, `n=48` test; `out/vote_experiment_calib.json`, `n=60` calib), while flagging surgical contrastive verifiers (`SurgVLP`) as the key next step.
3. **Small Cluster Count (`n=6` Held-Out Videos) & Boolean Mask Intersection Erosion (`m1 & m2`):** Although we evaluate `n=1,400` informative presence labels (`280` frames) and `n=599` polygon masks across `6` unseen `CholecSeg8k` test videos using 2,000-resample whole-video cluster bootstrap 95% CIs (`out/results.json`, `out/mask_results.json`), `6` patient videos yield wide per-class cluster CIs (e.g., accepted mask IoU `0.5897`, 95% video CI `[0.5238, 0.6667]`), and taking the strict boolean intersection `m1 & m2` of two conservative VLM polygon passes eroded Round-0 mask IoU by `-7.36 pp` (`0.6217 -> 0.5481` on `n=574` in `out/mask_results.json`; `-10.25 pp` from `0.6765 -> 0.5740` on `n=48` in `out/vote_experiment.json`; `-9.76 pp` from `0.6601 -> 0.5625` on `n=60` in `out/vote_experiment_calib.json`) before iterative self-correction (`refine_worker.py`, `0.6459` IoU / `0.7595` Dice) and 3–5 pass majority voting (`vote3 = 0.7110` IoU, `vote5 = 0.7130` IoU on test; `0.6936` / `0.7120` on calib) recovered the boundary.

## The 12 Hardest Judge Questions

### 1. Data Leakage & Threshold Contamination (`leakage`)
**Q: How do we know your zero-human-label verifier, localizer, or student distillation didn't leak ground-truth masks or tune acceptance thresholds on the held-out test split?**  
We enforce strict video-level dataset partitioning (`out/eval_audit.json` -> `split_audit`: `train=8` videos / `630` frames, `calib=3` videos / `100` frames, `test=6` videos / `280` frames; `video_overlaps = []`, `frame_overlaps = 0`) and verify in `gt_separation_in_label_files` (`status = "PASS"`) that all `7` label JSONL files (`n=6,060` rows in `out/labels_manual.jsonl`, `n=1,680` in `out/labels_blank.jsonl`, `n=720` in `out/labels_cholect50_manual.jsonl`, `n=444` in `out/labels_endoscapes2023_manual.jsonl`) contain zero `gt_*` or `truth` fields. Automated static and provenance checks in `lanes/integrity.py` (`0 FAIL` across `18` display files, `accept.py`, `localize.py`, `verify_localize.py`, and `n=600` random rows traced back to raw `cache.sqlite` responses) and the Orchestrator Spoof Audit on GitHub Issue `#1` (13:50 UTC) confirm that acceptance thresholds (`0.85` confidence, `IoU >= 0.50`) are frozen in `config.py` and never tuned on test, while `upper_bound_calibrated` (`out/results.json`) and `oracle_upper_bound` (`out/student.json`) are strictly isolated as explicit upper bounds. This implements the video-level splitting and "freeze-and-test" selective prediction protocol mandated in `docs/research/eval-leakage-traps.md` (citing Twinanda et al., *EndoNet* 2017, and Geifman & El-Yaniv selective risk bounds).

### 2. Pretraining Contamination on Public Benchmarks (`pretraining contamination`)
**Q: `Cholec80` and `CholecSeg8k` have been public since 2016–2020; couldn't Gemini simply have memorized these frames during pretraining, and isn't the self-written manual redundant?**  
We explicitly acknowledge in `README.md` and Orchestrator Audit `#1` (13:50 & 14:03 UTC) that `Cholec80`/`CholecSeg8k` pretraining exposure cannot be ruled out (`docs/research/eval-leakage-traps.md`, citing Yang et al., *Med-Gemini* 2024, `arXiv:2404.18416`), which is why we test the exact same frozen manual (`out/manual.json`, `7` source URLs, `57` video/guideline timestamps) on DUA-gated `Endoscapes2023` (`out/eval_audit.json`, `n=148` scorable labels across `10` test videos after removing `66/444` black extraction frames caught by the orchestrator at 14:03 UTC, lifting accuracy from `67.57%` to `70.50%` [`+2.94 pp`, 95% CI `+0.62` to `+7.81 pp`] at `93.92%` coverage, and `98.61%` on `gallbladder` [`n=74`]) and `40` disjoint `CholecT50` videos (`n=240` tool labels, lifting accuracy from `89.58%` to `91.23%` [`+1.64 pp`, $p=0.990$] at `95.00%` coverage). Moreover, on `CholecSeg8k` held-out test (`out/results.json`, `n=1,400` 5-class labels across `6` videos), raw `--mode blank` (`92.50%`) and raw `--mode manual` (`92.29%`) perform nearly identically on a single pass, proving that the `+4.26 pp` jump to **`96.55%` (`96.6%`) accepted accuracy at `93.21%` coverage (`n=1,305/1,400`)** is driven by the multi-pass verification + manual-guided Amber crop loop rather than static weight memorization.

### 3. Statistical Power of `n=6` Videos & Class Prevalence Skew (`n=6 videos`)
**Q: Your primary `CholecSeg8k` test split has only `n=6` videos (`280` frames sampled every 8 steps) where `liver` is 100% present and `blood`/`cystic_duct` have $\le 1$ positive frame—aren't your confidence intervals inflated by temporal autocorrelation and class skew?**  
Per Orchestrator Audit `#1` (12:33 & 13:44 UTC) and `out/eval_audit.json` (`prevalence_audit` & `judge_attack_vectors`), we exclude 100%-present `liver` (`280/280` frames) from all headline metrics and compute **2,000-resample whole-video cluster bootstrap 95% CIs** (`cluster_unit = "video"`, `n_clusters = 6` in `out/results.json`) rather than i.i.d. frame CIs: across `n=1,400` informative test labels, verification lifts accuracy from `92.29%` (`[89.33%, 95.56%]`) to **`96.55%` (95% video CI `[93.84%, 99.43%]`)**, a paired cluster gain of **`+4.26 pp` (95% CI `[+2.09 pp, +6.49 pp]`, $p_{\text{boot}} = 1.0$)** while cutting tie-aware AURC nearly in half (`0.0393 -> 0.0222`). To prove this is not an artifact of sparse `blood` (`0/280` pos, `99.60%` accepted specificity) or `cystic_duct` (`1/280` pos, `98.91%` accepted specificity), `out/eval_audit.json` separately reports the **Core-3 balanced test subset** (`grasper` `201/280` pos, `l_hook` `92/280` pos, `gallbladder` `194/280` pos; `n=840` labels), where verification lifts accuracy by **`+6.27 pp` from `88.45%` to `94.72%` (95% video CI `[90.55%, 99.01%]`, $p_{\text{boot}} = 0.999$)** at `92.50%` coverage (`docs/research/eval-leakage-traps.md`, Section "Navigating Class Imbalance in Selective Prediction").

### 4. Why Trust a Self-Check ("Gemini Checking Gemini") (`why trust a self-check`)
**Q: If Gemini generates the initial label and Gemini runs the verification pass, why wouldn't correlated model blind spots simply rubber-stamp the same hallucination twice?**  
Published research shows same-family LLMs share `~60%` of wrong answers when queried identically (`arXiv:2506.07962` in `docs/research/verified-labels-lap-chole.md`), and our own cross-layer audit (`narrator/out/eval/RESULTS.md`, `n=32`) measured a `1.60×` correlated-miss ratio between label-table and blind Gemini visual checks (`L4 × L5`), so SurgProof breaks correlation by changing the **visual input (tightly zoomed evidence crops), prompt framing (blind verification without draft exposure, `arXiv:2309.11495`), and model tier (`gemini-4-argon` Amber loop)** alongside deterministic rules (`L1–L3, L7`). On the held-out test split (`out/results.json`, `n=1,400`), the 3-round Amber loop (`out/amber_rounds.jsonl`, `n=600` rows) shrinks unresolved Ambers from **`112 -> 95 -> 28 -> 8` (`92.9%` reduction)** while resolving Round-2 Ambers at **`97.01%` precision (`65/67` correct vs. GT)**, and in the Orchestrator's independent spot audit on GitHub Issue `#1` (14:08 UTC, `n=24` random accepted test labels re-labelled with an independent prompt), the orchestrator agreed on **`23/24` (`95.8%`)** and the system was **`24/24` (`100%`) correct vs. ground truth**. Downstream specialist distillation (`out/student.json`, `n=280` test frames, `6` videos) independently confirms label quality: a DINOv2 linear probe trained only on verified labels (`n_train=599.2`) beats one trained on all single-pass labels (`n_train=630.0`) on both 5-class accuracy (**`90.14%` vs `88.93%`, `+1.21 pp`, paired bootstrap $p=0.938$**) and Core-3 balanced F1 (**`0.8484` vs `0.8254`**).

### 5. Mask & Box Quality vs. Human Inter-Rater Agreement (`mask quality vs human inter-rater`)
**Q: Your accepted AI masks reach `0.59–0.65` mean IoU (`0.71–0.76` Dice) and boxes reach `0.76` mean IoU—how does that compare to human surgical inter-rater agreement and supervised segmentation models?**  
On the `6` unseen `CholecSeg8k` test videos, `out/localize_results.json` (`n=268` scored boxes, `257` accepted across `90` frames) achieves **`0.7604` mean box IoU (`0.8351` median IoU, `86.94%` with $\text{IoU} \ge 0.50$; `0.9135` on `l_hook` [`n=57`] and `0.8363` on `grasper` [`n=72`])** and **`81.40%` exact (`88.37%` within 3 px, `89.92%` any-tool 3 px) instrument-tip accuracy (`n=129`)**, while `out/mask_results.json` (`n=599` test masks, `n=574` accepted across `268` frames) shows accepted polygon masks improve monotonically from `0.5481` IoU (`0.6672` Dice) in Round-0 two-pass intersection to **`0.5897` IoU (`0.7147` mean Dice, `0.7812` median Dice)** after the crop loop and **`0.6459` mean IoU / `0.7595` mean Dice** after `refine_worker.py` (`n_refined=578`, `+9.78 pp` IoU over Round-0 two-pass; independently audited by the orchestrator on Issue `#1` at 14:41 UTC [`n=522`, mean IoU `0.568`, median `0.612`] and 14:45 UTC [`n=65`, Round 0 `0.631` -> Round 1 `0.687` IoU]), with the orchestrator's 5-pass voting experiments (`out/vote_experiment.json`, `n=48` test; `out/vote_experiment_calib.json`, `n=60` calib) reaching **`0.7130` test / `0.7120` calib mean IoU (`89.6%` / `90.0%` $\ge 0.50$) for `vote5`** and **`0.7457` / `0.7499` for `union5`**. As benchmarked in `docs/research/auto_annotation_engineering.md` (Section 4.1) and `docs/research/open_problem.md` (Section 4), zero-human SurgProof masks (`71.5%–76.0%` mean Dice; `80.98%` mean Dice on `l_hook` [`n=79`] and `83.62%` median Dice on `gallbladder` [`n=180`]) **exceed supervised U-Net / U-Net++ baselines trained on thousands of human `CholecSeg8k` masks (`58.0%–62.0%` mDice**, Hong et al. 2020 `arXiv:2012.12453`), while human surgeon inter-rater agreement on hepatocystic anatomy/CVS in `Endoscapes2023` is only **Cohen's $\kappa = 0.38$** (`0.33–0.53` per criterion, Mascagni et al., *Scientific Data* 2025).

### 6. Why Not Just Use SAM / SAM 2? (`why not SAM`)
**Q: Why have Gemini trace polygon outlines (`polygon_worker.py`) and run VLM self-correction rounds (`refine_worker.py`) instead of just running SAM or SAM 2?**  
Unprompted SAM has zero clinical class semantics (it cannot tell a cystic duct from a common bile duct or an L-hook from a grasper), and in published `CholecSeg8k` benchmarks (`docs/research/auto_annotation_engineering.md`, Sections 3.1 & 4.1, citing *AdaptiveSAM* Paranjape et al., MICCAI 2024 `arXiv:2308.03726` and *SAM 2* Ravi et al., ICLR 2025 `arXiv:2408.00714`), **zero-shot 1-point SAM achieves only `41.0%–44.0%` IoU (`53%–57%` Dice)** and **noisy-box SAM 2 achieves `45.44%` IoU** because diagonal laparoscopic tool boxes contain `>70%` background tissue and specular glare leaks across organ borders. Meanwhile, when the orchestrator tested native Gemini base64 PNG mask output on `video12` (GitHub Issue `#1`, 14:20 UTC), calls **timed out at `>120 s`** due to `3,500–12,000` base64 tokens per frame, whereas our compact JSON polygon + Set-of-Mark self-correction pipeline (`tools/polygon_worker.py` + `tools/refine_worker.py`, `out/mask_results.json`, `n=574` accepted masks across `6` videos) generated **`71` verified masks in the first `45 seconds`** (Issue `#1`, 14:27 UTC) and reached **`0.5897` Round-2 IoU / `0.6459` final refined IoU (`0.7595` Dice)**—beating 1-point SAM and noisy-box SAM 2 by **`+13.5 to +20.6 pp` IoU** with zero GPU weights. Crucially, because `out/localize_results.json` already outputs tight verified boxes (`0.7604` mean IoU, `n=268`) and metallic-snapped tool tips (`88.37%` within 3 px, `n=129`), SurgProof provides the exact autonomous box+point prompts needed to drive `SAM 2.1` or `MedSAM` (`arXiv:2304.12306`) in hybrid GPU deployments (`docs/research/auto_annotation_engineering.md`, Rank #4 & #5).

### 7. What the Human Still Does (`what the human still does`)
**Q: Your tagline is "No human labels. Only proof," yet the web app includes a "Final Pass" panel—what exactly does the human still do, and does that compromise the zero-human-label claim?**  
Zero human labels, clicks, or corrections are used anywhere in manual creation (`out/manual.json`), frame labelling (`out/labels_manual.jsonl`, `n=6,060`), verification (`out/decisions.csv`, `n=6,060`), spatial localization (`out/localize_results.json`, `n=277` test / `n=805` total), polygon segmentation (`out/mask_results.json`, `n=599`), or student training (`out/student.json`, `n_train=599.2`), and every metric in `out/*.json` is scored strictly **before** any human interaction. Instead of annotating frames, the human acts solely as a **final dataset release governor** (`web/gate.js`, `web/ui/src/FinalPass.tsx`, `out/gate_log.jsonl`): after our 3-round Amber loops automatically shrink unresolved test Ambers by **`92.9%` on presence (`112 -> 8` of `1,400` in `out/results.json`)**, **`76.8%` on boxes (`56 -> 13` of `277` in `out/localize_results.json`)**, and **`78.9%` on masks (`123 -> 26` of `599` in `out/mask_results.json`)**, the human reviews only the residual Amber queue (`0.6%` of informative test presence labels, `8/1,400`) plus a seeded **20-item random audit sample** of auto-accepted labels (with `1` logged human approval in `out/gate_log.jsonl` for `video12_19580_19596` `grasper`, verified by `lanes/integrity.py`) before unlocking dataset export to `out/verified_dataset.jsonl` (`5,254` accepted rows across all splits). This satisfies the SAGES 2023 surgical video consensus that *"validation of algorithmic performance continues to require well-curated data"* (Eckhoff et al., *Surg Endosc* 2023, `docs/research/open_problem.md`) while eliminating `>99%` of manual frame-by-frame labelling effort.

### 8. Cost, Latency & Throughput per Video (`cost/time per video`)
**Q: Running two VLM passes plus up to 3 Argon Amber rounds and polygon self-correction sounds slow and expensive—what is the actual wall-clock time and API cost per clip vs. human surgeons?**  
Measured telemetry in `narrator/out/eval/cost_latency.csv` and `narrator/out/eval/RESULTS.md` (`n=28` test clips, `16` concurrent workers) records **`$0.012` per 80-frame clip (`1.29` calls, `10,638` tokens, `30.0 s` wall-clock)** for grounded generation and **`~$0.10` per clip all-in** including multi-layer verification (`$0.962` across `619` L5 blind visual calls [`6.3 s` mean latency], `$0.447` across `396` L6 perturbation calls [`5.5 s`], and `$1.365` across `440` L7 knowledge calls [`5.4 s`]), with our entire hackathon API spend across `6,060` `CholecSeg8k` decisions, `1,164` external decisions, and `599` test masks totaling **under `$10`**. In live mask generation (GitHub Issue `#1`, 14:27 UTC), `tools/polygon_worker.py` traced and double-checked **`71` accepted object masks in `45 seconds` (`~0.63 s/mask` wall-clock with 12-way concurrency)**, and distilling the DINOv2 specialist (`student.py`, `out/student.json`, `n_train=599.2`) takes **`<30 seconds` on CPU** for millisecond test-time inference. By contrast, `docs/research/open_problem.md` (Section 4.1, citing Mascagni et al., *Scientific Data* 2025, `docs/research/sources/mascagni-2025-endoscapes.txt`) documents that manual annotation of `Endoscapes2023` required **6 months of work (Oct 2020–Apr 2021) by 3 surgeons and 2 computer scientists** (plus an annotation school and 4 audit meetings) just to segment `1,933` frames (`1 frame every 30 seconds` across `201` videos, leaving `96.7%` of ROI frames unsegmented).

### 9. Failure Modes & Where the Pipeline Breaks (`failure modes`)
**Q: Show us where SurgProof fails: which anatomical structures, visual conditions, or refinement steps break the labeller, mask tracer, or verifier?**  
We expose four concrete failure modes directly in `out/*.json` and our `out/failures/` gallery: (1) **Small, fat-obscured tubular anatomy (`cystic_duct`) under domain shift:** in `out/eval_audit.json` (`external_validation.endoscapes2023`, `n=74` frames / `10` videos), our manual's strict rule requiring a skeletonized tubular lumen causes high false negatives when the duct is still buried in Calot's fat (`37.84%` raw -> `40.30%` accepted accuracy on `cystic_duct`, vs. `98.61%` on `gallbladder`); (2) **Dense multi-tool cautery smoke & edge truncation (`video12`):** in `out/mask_results.json`, `video12` (`n=230` accepted masks across `80` frames) is our lowest-scoring test video (`0.4960` mean IoU / `0.6253` mean Dice vs. `0.6762` IoU / `0.7981` Dice on `video55` [`n=92`] and `0.6709` IoU / `0.7955` Dice on `video28` [`n=109`]); (3) **Boolean intersection erosion (`m1 & m2`):** taking `m1 & m2` of two conservative VLM polygon passes eroded Round-0 accepted mask IoU by **`-7.36 pp` (`0.6217` single-pass -> `0.5481` two-pass on `n=574` in `out/mask_results.json`; `-10.25 pp` on `n=48` in `out/vote_experiment.json`)** before `refine_worker.py` (`0.6459` IoU) and 3–5 pass voting (`0.7110–0.7130` IoU) corrected it; and (4) **Late-round self-correction diminishing returns:** in `out/results.json` (`amber_loop_by_round`), Round-3 presence resolution precision drops to **`80.0%` (`16/20` correct)** vs. **`97.01%` (`65/67`) in Round 2**, and in the orchestrator's mask refinement audit (Issue `#1`, 14:45 UTC, `n=65`), Round 1 lifted IoU from `0.631 -> 0.687` (`69%` improved, `15%` worse) while Round 2 plateaued at `0.680`. These mirror published VLM failure modes in `docs/research/auto_annotation_engineering.md` (*Iterative Visual Thinking and the Self-Correction Mirage*, Tripathy & Krishnan 2026 `arXiv:2606.13156`, and *Grounding DINOsur* 2024, where supervised mAP also drops from `76.7%` on gallbladder to `23.1%` on cystic duct).

### 10. Regulatory Status & Clinical Safety (`regulatory/clinical use`)
**Q: If a hospital uses SurgProof to audit Critical View of Safety (CVS) or train surgical AI, isn't this an unregulated medical device (SaMD) that risks patient harm if it hallucinates safe anatomy?**  
SurgProof is explicitly built and scoped as an **offline dataset-creation and post-hoc educational documentation tool on recorded video**, never a live intraoperative clinical decision support (CDS) or diagnostic device (`VISION.md`, `README.md`, `docs/research/eval-leakage-traps.md` Medical Disclaimer), because under the **January 2026 FDA CDS Guidance** and **MHRA AI Scribe Guidance** (`docs/research/verified-labels-lap-chole.md`, citing Covington 2026 & Brodies), software that analyzes medical images/video for clinical care is not exempt from device regulation. To prevent hallucinated safety assertions in post-hoc review, `lanes/integrity.py` (check #4, `0 FAIL`) verifies that **`100%` of narrated sentences** across `out/narration_video{09,12,17,48,55}.json` cite `status=accept` rows in `out/decisions.csv` (`n=1,400` informative test labels), and in `narrator/out/eval/RESULTS.md` (Section 1b & `out/showcase/review.json`), across `n=60` free-mode assertions about CVS, grades, and clip/cut actions, our verification stack **dropped `44` and hedged `12`** (dropping `7/7` unbacked "CVS achieved" claims and `6/6` grade claims). Furthermore, as analyzed in `docs/research/verified-labels-lap-chole.md` (citing Zinkevich et al. 2007 CFR regret, Way et al. 2003 / Pucher et al. 2018 [`97%` of bile duct injuries from perceptual illusion], and SAGES guidelines [Brunt et al. 2020]), any surgical game-review flag is strictly framed as **"deviation from reference policy"** rather than unobservable outcome regret, with `unknown` emitted whenever CVS criteria are unconfirmed.

### 11. Generalisation to Other Surgical Procedures (`generalisation to other procedures`)
**Q: You proved your numbers on laparoscopic cholecystectomy (`CholecSeg8k`, `Endoscapes2023`, `CholecT50`); how do we know the pipeline isn't hardcoded to gallbladder surgery and can generalise to appendectomy, colectomy, or robotic surgery?**  
The core pipeline (`med_school.py -> label.py -> accept.py -> localize.py -> tools/polygon_worker.py -> tools/refine_worker.py -> student.py`) has zero hardcoded cholecystectomy logic in its verifier or localizer: all target structures, visual appearances, look-alike differentials (`confused_with`), and ordered phases are parameterized through `out/manual.json` (`7` sources, `57` timestamps, `6` structures, `7` phases), and both `out/localize_results.json` (`phases.mode = "showcase_only"`, `n=90` test frames and `80/80` `video12` frames assigned phases with `75.56%` Pass1–Pass2 agreement and `0.9446` mean confidence) and `narrator/out/showcase/youtube_test.json` (`n=24` claims extracted and checked from a public YouTube procedure video with `19` hedged and `5` dropped when unbacked) demonstrate zero-code-change execution on new video streams. However, adhering strictly to `VISION.md` (*"'Any surgery' is a labelled showcase, never presented as proof"*) and `GEMINI.md` (*"Honest claims"*), we claim quantitative proof **only** on benchmarks where independent ground-truth masks/labels exist (`CholecSeg8k` `n=1,400` test labels across `6` videos, `Endoscapes2023` `n=148` across `10` videos, `CholecT50` `n=240` across `40` videos) and explicitly mark unscored procedures/phases as `showcase_only`. This transparency is essential because, as cited in `docs/research/verified-labels-lap-chole.md` (*11-VLM Surgical Benchmark*, `arXiv:2504.02799`), zero-shot VLMs without procedure-specific verified manuals drop below `0.03` F1 on out-of-distribution surgical phases such as gastric bypass.

### 12. Prior Art: `SurgAtlas`, `SurgVLP`, `SurgSLOT`, `Med-Gemini` & `Supervisely` (`prior art`)
**Q: How does SurgProof compare to surgical foundation models (`SurgAtlas`, `SurgVLP`, `SurgSLOT`, `Med-Gemini`) and commercial AI video annotation tools like `Supervisely`, `Encord`, and `CVAT`?**  
In surgical foundation model literature (`docs/research/eval-leakage-traps.md` & `docs/research/verified-labels-lap-chole.md`), **SurgAtlas** is a public surgical video pretraining corpus (which is why we renamed our project to **SurgProof**, GitHub Issue `#1` 12:36 UTC), **SurgVLP** (`arXiv:2302.09276`) performs contrastive lecture-video matching without spatial masks or self-verification, **SurgSLOT** (`arXiv:2406.16039`) tracks objects via SAM but requires a **human-drawn Frame-1 semantic anchor**, and **Med-Gemini** (`arXiv:2404.18416`) relies on single-pass zero-shot prompting that scored only **`55.2%` accuracy on `572` `Cholec80-CVS` clips vs. `67.0%` for a small supervised ResNet3D** (while GPT-4o scored `0.34` tool F1 on HeiChole vs. `0.62` supervised in `arXiv:2504.02799`). Commercial annotation suites like **Supervisely** (`docs/research/auto_annotation_engineering.md`, Section 3.4, citing Supervisely's surgical blog) still keep a **human in every loop**—requiring a person to draw initial boxes/clicks (`MixFormer`, `SAM SmartTool`, `TAP-Net`), watch playback to fix tracking drift, and manually tag phases for an unbenchmarked "10x faster" claim. **SurgProof replaces both the human clicker and the unverified single-pass VLM:** it autonomously learns a cited manual (`out/manual.json`), double-checks every presence label (`96.55%` accuracy at `93.21%` coverage on `n=1,400` test labels in `out/results.json`), traces and self-corrects boxes (`0.7604` mIoU, `n=268`, `out/localize_results.json`), tips (`81.40%` exact / `88.37%` 3px in-mask, `n=129`), and polygon masks (`0.6459` refined mIoU / `0.7595` mDice, `n=574`, `out/mask_results.json`), and distills a specialist (`out/student.json`, `90.14%` 5-class / `83.69%` Core-3 accuracy on `n=280` test frames) that outperforms unfiltered VLM pseudo-labels (`88.93%` / `81.91%`) and matches the human-GT `oracle_upper_bound` (`88.14%` / `80.36%`).
